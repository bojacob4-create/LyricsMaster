2️⃣ Validate /top genre charts

The /top command should represent popular songs for a genre today, not historical classics.

Example problem observed:

/top soul returned:

John Legend — All of Me

This song is more than 10 years old, which suggests the system might be pulling:

• generic artist discographies
• static examples
• popularity-based picks
• or random songs tagged with the genre

Instead, /top should ideally represent recent popular songs in that genre.

Please verify:

• How /top currently selects songs
• Whether the genre results come from a chart source or another dataset
• Whether the data represents recent popularity

If possible, adjust the selection logic so /top prioritizes:

• recent charting songs
• currently popular tracks
• or trending tracks within that genre

The goal is for /top pop, /top rap, /top kpop, /top soul, etc. to feel current and relevant.
